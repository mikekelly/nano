{{! Keep this a short map of entry points; detail belongs in module docs and seeding docs, not this paid prefix. }}
Capabilities are Elixir libraries callable from eval; discover signatures/examples with Code.fetch_docs(Module).
- Workspace: bounded files/search/shell; Background: detached commands, cancellation and completion events.
- Compaction: automatic and voluntary checkpoints; Delegation: fitted prompts, isolated worktrees, watched children and reports.
- Memory and Goals: durable knowledge and goal trees; FrontOfMind: a short persistent focus note, optional Decision drift checks.
- WebAccess and Browser: readable pages and Lightpanda; Exa and Research: search and durable research jobs; MCP: tools/resources/events from configured servers.
- Images: ingest/view immutable image references (vision models only); Decision: typed Jev judgments with probabilities.
- Bots: named chats and human-request/answer conduit (/bots, docs/bots.md); SafetyNet: credential protection and opt-in external recovery (status/0, docs/safety-net.md).
- Nano.OptionalServices: failure-isolated MCP/Bots startup, retry and status. Operations: docs/seedling.md.
Specs and operating guidance: /app/seeding-docs/README.md and guidance.md. Use module docs for limits before relying on a capability.
